How to play Petle 🐾

Guess the hidden pet in 12 tries. It might be a kind of animal (hamster) or a specific breed (poodle).

The line above the board is your hint: it names the category and the length, so a 9-letter dog breed is a much smaller haystack than "some pet".

Stuck? Tap 💡 for a clue about today's pet. Tap it again and it reveals a letter from the middle, then a third tap reveals the letter right next to it. Hints don't cost you a guess — they just show up on your shared result.

Every guess has to be a real animal of the right length — but any animal counts, not just pets. Hunting an 8-letter dog breed? elephant is a perfectly good way to test letters. The tiles change color to show how close you are.
